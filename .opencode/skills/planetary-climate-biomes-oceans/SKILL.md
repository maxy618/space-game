# Planetary Climate Biomes Oceans

1. Research combined effects of star/orbit, atmosphere/pressure/composition, albedo, rotation, axial tilt, gravity, geology, volatiles and water; record uncertainty.
2. Separate planet climate, regional biome, local terrain, weather front and transient event.
3. Build a dependency graph; do not assign biome independently per tile.
4. Use deterministic spatial fields/seeds for continuous regions/chunks.
5. Design macro geography: ranges, basins, deserts, ice caps, ocean basins, islands/coastlines; then regional materials.
6. Make long ocean crossings feel vast and preserve waves/coasts across chunks.
7. Use shared data for visible terrain and collision.
8. Label game abstraction/fiction separately from science.

Avoid random biome tiles, every biome on every planet, noise-only geology or chunk seams.
