# Rover Surface Vehicles

1. Inspect terrain/collision, landing and persistence.
2. Research simple 2D handling for acceleration, steering, wheel/track contact, traction and suspension.
3. Connect behavior to slopes/materials/obstacles.
4. Implement deployment, driving, return, position ownership and save/load.
5. Use rover-specific camera/controls; support simultaneous input and pointer cancellation.
6. Test flat, rocky, sandy, icy, coastal and chunk-boundary routes.
7. Play ship→rover→explore→ship through Playwright.

Avoid teleporting vehicle positions, invisible collision mismatch, ship reskin or unrecoverable traps.
