# Astronomy Evidence Catalogue

1. Prefer NASA/ESA/JPL/IAU, NASA Exoplanet Archive, Gaia and peer-reviewed work. Record retrieval dates.
2. Distinguish confirmed, candidate/debated, theoretical/hypothetical and fictional.
3. Separate objects, remnants, transient events, descriptive properties and model categories.
4. Record units, uncertainty, measurement versus estimate and source for each claim.
5. Investigate unsettled classification boundaries; do not promote hypotheses to confirmed classes.
6. Translate evidence into generation rules/player-facing explanations without claiming a full scientific simulation.
7. Validate schema and visible evidence labels.

Avoid invented formal classes, unsourced precision or synthetic game worlds represented as observed catalogue objects.
