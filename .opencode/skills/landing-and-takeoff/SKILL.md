# Landing And Takeoff

1. Derive suitability from slope, roughness, obstacles, material, weather/wind, ship capability and approach state.
2. Provide clear contextual readiness cues with minimal HUD.
3. Require controlled approach/descent and preserve exact coordinates.
4. Implement touchdown/stabilization/takeoff as real state transitions.
5. Support abort/recovery and explain unsupported surfaces.
6. Connect landing to rover, discoveries and space exit.
7. Play repeat routes under varied conditions and save/reload.

Avoid one-button teleport, landing at a different point, unexplained launch failure or stuck landed state.
