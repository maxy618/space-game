# Ecosystem Art And Behavior

1. Research ecology/astrobiology for inspiration; clearly label alien organisms as fictional content.
2. Define species identity, size, silhouette, habitat, niche, movement, activity, social/avoidance patterns and population.
3. Design silhouette first, then anatomy, materials, fine detail, sound and secondary motion; avoid recolored clones.
4. Tie placement to climate/biome/terrain/water/light/weather with stable seeds.
5. Implement meaningful states: roam, feed, hover, burrow, cluster, retreat, investigate, display etc.
6. React to player proximity and environmental change.
7. Add observe/scan/discover and journal persistence.
8. Play encounters; inspect ecosystem composition and close animation.

Avoid static props, same body plan recolored, random habitat placement, false alien-life claims and gore as a substitute for atmosphere.
