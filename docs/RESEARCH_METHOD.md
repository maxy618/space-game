# Research method

Formulate the question, search authoritative sources, compare alternatives, record publication/retrieval dates, cite exact claims and state uncertainty. Prefer official documentation, standards, peer-reviewed work, NASA/ESA/JPL/IAU and direct gameplay. Keep observation, fact, inference, engineering choice, game abstraction and fiction separate. For parallel agents, delegate independent tracks with a common template; lead verifies and synthesizes. Never claim unrun tools/subagents.
